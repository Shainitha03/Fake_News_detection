About the Project & Machine Learning Model

This system uses classical Natural Language Processing (NLP). Raw news articles are cleaned (lowercased, punctuation and stopwords removed, and stemmed with PorterStemmer). Text is then vectorized using TF-IDF (50,000 unigram/bigram features) and evaluated by a fitted Logistic Regression classifier.

The model achieved 97.4% test accuracy on its training dataset (ISOT Fake & True News).

⚠️ Note: Predictions reflect statistical linguistic patterns from historical news data and should not be used as infallible factual verification. Unseen topics or brief sentences may produce incorrect classifications.
